## 6. Sat 31 Oct 2026, 10:00 IST: MyThreadPay

Piece-rate wages are easy to get wrong when they're worked out by hand at the end of the week.

MyThreadPay records pieces and attendance as the work is done, keeps every rate with its date, and works out the week's pay. The owner approves the week and it is locked, so every rupee traces back to the work.

In English and Tamil. Now in early access: https://wohnmohr.com/products/mythreadpay
