wohnmohr is a product company: a growing suite of focused software for the jobs people do every week, and nothing else.

Products we run today:
• Split Biller: split bills in ₹ and settle with UPI. No signup, no app download. splitbiller.com
• NicheLinq: one link, QR code and shop screen for a small business's offers, each with a button to pay, order, book or call. nichelinq.com
• ChargeMyAI (early access): add AI to any web app with one script tag. Users pay per reply over UPI, and the developer earns a share. chargemyai.com
• MyThreadPay (early access): piece-rate and daily wages for garment, carton and construction units, counted and traceable. mythreadpay.com

How we build:
• One job, done end to end.
• No wall in front of the door: you try it before you commit.
• You approve before anything goes out.
• Money goes straight to the right person.

We are working on more products that make everyday life easier for ordinary people. Founded in 2025, rooted in Trichy, Tamil Nadu.
