# Surface brief: homepage (src/pages/index.astro)

Scope: wohnmohr.com homepage plus shared chrome (nav, footer, buttons, tokens) that every page inherits. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and action: product users click through to the right product; partners and investors leave convinced wohnmohr ships and runs real products. Product company only: no client work, services or pricing anywhere. Proof: real screenshots of each product site in public/products/, each product's published features, prices and status. No invented numbers, customers or quotes.

Constraints: keep routes, slugs, data-track event names, Calendly/Tally/WhatsApp contact. Logo mark shape kept.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the category standard for a product company, played straight at Linear/Stripe/Apple/37signals craft: the products are the page. Refuses glow blobs, dark AI hero, fake div product UIs, eyebrows and identical feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: near-white ground (#FBFBFA), ink #0F1012, graphite secondary, hairlines at 10% ink; one company accent, cobalt #2B5BFF. Each product tile carries a pale tint of that product's own brand colour and its real screenshot. Mona Sans for everything (tight display tracking), Geist Mono only for code. Buttons full-pill; frames and tiles 16px radius; screenshots in a thin-bordered frame with a soft, tinted shadow.

STORY: read the headline (what wohnmohr is), see the real products, pick one; read how we build in plain words; contact at the end for feedback, partnerships and investors.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left-aligned headline over two lines at ~88px, 18-word subline, ink pill "See the products" plus outline pill "How we build"; below, a wide frame of real product screenshots begins inside the viewport at 1440x900.

FORM: category standard (canon), chosen by the user after the signwriter pick was rejected; seed key f2e1f43f. Signature interaction: product tiles lift their screenshot on hover and the tile tint deepens; entrance is one staggered rise of hero text and the screenshot strip. Reduced motion: static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
